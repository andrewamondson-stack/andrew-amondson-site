CV

Andrew Amondson is an American-born artist and filmmaker based in Berlin. His practice spans large-scale kinetic installation, soundscape composition, documentary filmmaking, and Walking With — a walking methodology developed across Studio Olafur Eliasson, Netflix, and the Berlinale before becoming its own art-and-science practice. He is a member of Studio Olafur Eliasson, a collaborator with EER, and a Fellow of the Royal Society for Arts.
